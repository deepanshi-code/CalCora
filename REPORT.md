## Limits of this validation

- The reference shares the expression *tree* with the generator, so tree-generation bugs are not caught, only evaluator bugs.
- Derivatives and integrals are checked numerically at sampled points, not proven equal symbolically.
- Random trees are not a substitute for real student input; see `docs/USABILITY_TEST.md`.
